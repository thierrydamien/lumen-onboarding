Onboarding Assistant · team hub

The three tools behind Lumen client onboarding. Bookmark this page rather than the individual URLs.

Create a client link Sales & CS

Fill in the client's details, attach or paste their brief, and generate the personalised onboarding link to send them. This is the starting point for every client.

 Sessions dashboard Proserv

Every client session: what they captured, their requirements document, who is stalled and needs a nudge, and the time saved across the team. Needs the Proserv access token.

 Open the assistant Demo & testing

The client-facing chat with no client attached, so nothing is pre-filled. Use it to demo the experience or test a change. A real client always gets their own link from Sales instead.

A note on the last one: opening the assistant here starts an unseeded session, so it will not know a company or contact and the brief it produces is not tied to a client. For anything real, generate a link from Sales.

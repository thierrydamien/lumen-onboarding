Setting up a client

1 Create their link Sales & CS Add the client's details, attach or paste their brief, and generate the personalised link to send them.

The client works through the conversation on their own link, in their own language, whenever suits them. It takes them about 15 minutes and they can pause and pick it up on any device.

2 Review what came back Proserv Every session and its requirements document, who has stalled and needs a nudge, and the time saved across the team. Asks for the Proserv access token.

Demo & testing

Open the assistant with no client attached Nothing is pre-filled and any brief it produces is not tied to a client, so it is for showing the experience or trying a change. Real clients always get their own link from step one.
